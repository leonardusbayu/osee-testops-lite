YOUR NEXT STEP

Find the right information.

How do I read my registration?

Draft means the saved record is not confirmed. Confirmed is the saved registration status; it is not evidence of a verified payment. Attendance and results have their own separate statuses.

When can I join my session?

Check the schedule and wait until the host is marked ready. Joining and check-in are not implemented here; follow your organiser's instructions.

Why don't I see a score or download?

No score rendering or private document delivery is implemented. The result view reports actual saved release state, never a fabricated score.

How can I make a change?

Contact your organiser using your existing channel. This local portal has no booking, payment, rescheduling or support-message submission service.
